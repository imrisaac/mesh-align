Mesh Align

scan → datum frame → CAD

STL, OBJ or PLY. You can also drop a file on the view.

How it works
  1. Add a Plane and click the flat face you want on the XY plane. Each click grows a patch across the flat area.
  2. Add a Circle and click 3+ points around the hole's edge, or a Cylinder and click patches on the hole wall.
  3. The datum frame fills in as you go: first plane → Z axis, first hole → origin, next hole or plane → X direction. Change any of it in step 2.
  4. Download the aligned STL and import it into Onshape. Its origin and Top plane now match your features.
